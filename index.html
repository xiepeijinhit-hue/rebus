01 / THE IDEA

💡 When pictures become words

看见线索，听见含义

Can a vision–language model do more than recognize what is in a picture? A rebus hides an expression in visual clues: objects, counts, actions, attributes and spatial relations whose names suggest sounds and meanings.

BiRebus connects visual understanding with lexical choice and phonological reasoning across English and Chinese. It pairs constrained emoji puzzles with open-ended pictorial scenes, exposing a gap between seeing the clues and solving the wordplay.

02 / THE APPROACH

🧠 See it. Sound it. Solve it.

Structured Rebus Reasoning

A lightweight prompting strategy separates visual grounding from phonological imagination. No parameter updates. No additional training data.

Visual grounding

📝 What these results do — and do not — establish

The paper and local artifacts differ in coverage, selection and request settings. This public snapshot includes 374/400 empty screening predictions; empty responses are not verified reasoning mistakes. Human-study records are not released. Local audit scores use all expected items, with failures receiving no end-to-end credit. These comparisons alone do not establish a causal prompt effect. Full data audit ↗
